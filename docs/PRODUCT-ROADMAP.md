# ORBI Condo — Product Roadmap

## Product thesis
ORBI Condo enters through a narrow, visible problem: residents cannot easily understand the maintenance state of their community. The first product must make that information beautiful, clear and useful before expanding into administration.

## Phase 0 — PC2 Showcase
Goal: create the “I want this in my condominium” effect.

- Premium responsive identity for Parque Ciudadano II.
- Maintenance systems, frequencies, provider and visible status.
- Annual calendar concept.
- Provider cards with verified/unverified separation.
- Certifications and expiry visibility.
- System detail drawer.
- No authentication, billing or database yet.

## Phase 1 — Maintenance Operations
Build only after the showcase is validated by real users.

- Asset register.
- Maintenance history.
- Work orders.
- Evidence photos and reports.
- Contract and supplier records.
- Certificate repository and expiry alerts.
- QR access per technical asset.

## Phase 2 — Community Operations
- Resident communications.
- Requests and incidents.
- Shared documents.
- Common-area information.
- Service provider coordination.

## Phase 3 — Administration Platform
- Units and residents.
- Budgets and expenses.
- Contracts.
- Collections/integrations where legally and operationally appropriate.
- Portfolio-level operational dashboard.

## Phase 4 — ORBI Condo Operations Center
Multi-community view for a geographically concentrated portfolio. The differentiator is not only software: it is operational traceability, technical maintenance discipline, supplier performance and transparency for residents.

## Guardrails
1. Do not invent dates, providers, certifications or maintenance evidence.
2. Keep community data separate from presentation components.
3. Build the smallest real capability that solves the next validated problem.
4. PC2 is the first implementation, not the limit of the product architecture.
5. Visual quality is part of the product: clarity first, spectacle second, both required.
